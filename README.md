# Deep Generative Learning

Laboratory exercises and implementations for the **Deep Learning 2** course at the [Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (FER)](https://www.fer.unizg.hr/), University of Zagreb.

**Academic Year:** 2026/2027  
**Course:** [Deep Learning 2](https://www.fer.unizg.hr/predmet/dubuce2)  
**ECTS:** 5

## Course Overview

The course explores the theoretical foundations and practical applications of **deep generative models**, focusing on learning complex data distributions and generating realistic samples.

### Topics Covered

- Mathematical foundations of generative modeling
- Unsupervised learning and representation learning
- Autoregressive models
- Autoencoders and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)
- Normalizing Flows
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)
- Energy-Based Models (EBMs)
- Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBMs)
- Deep Belief Networks (DBNs)

## Repository Contents

This repository primarily contains **laboratory exercises**, including implementations, experiments, and evaluations of deep generative models covered throughout the course.
